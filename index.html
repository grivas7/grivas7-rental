📵 DIGITAL DETOX

A little less screen time.

There is no TV in the apartment — intentionally. 😊 Think of it as a small digital detox. Instead of spending your evenings in front of a screen, enjoy a glass of wine, a walk by the sea, dinner in Vonitsa or a quiet evening at home.

Your holiday deserves a little less screen time and a little more Greece. 🇬🇷

📍 YOUR LOCAL GUIDE

Just scan, choose and enjoy.

As soon as you arrive, you'll find QR codes for the Wi-Fi and our local guide. It includes beaches, restaurants and cafés, places worth visiting, day trips and GPS directions. Choose how many minutes you want to drive, pick a destination or beach, and you're ready to go.

Your holiday, made simple.

🏖️ OUR BEACH

Easy days by the sea.

The beach near the apartment has free sun loungers available for everyone, so you can enjoy a relaxing day by the sea without paying for a sunbed. Water shoes are recommended, as there are some sea urchins in the area.

If you don't have water shoes or space in your suitcase, there are local shops nearby where you can find water shoes and other useful holiday items.

Pack your clothes. We'll take care of the rest.
